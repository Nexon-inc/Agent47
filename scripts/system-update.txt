⚠️ Where you need to be careful (important)

This is where I’m going to tighten it up.

🚨 1️⃣ Legal / trust risk: “Replaces $X/month human role”

This phrasing is powerful but borderline dangerous if taken literally.

Why?

People may expect human-level autonomy

Regulators + enterprise buyers hate absolute replacement claims

🔧 Fix (small wording tweak, big safety)

Instead of:

“Replaces $5,000/month human role”

Use:

“Covers the core workload of a $5,000/month role”

or

“Delivers the operational output of a $5,000/month role”

Same impact. Less liability. More credibility.

🚨 2️⃣ Performance Guarantees are too hard

These lines are risky:

“99.9% on-time delivery”

“<60 seconds response”

“100% follow-ups”

If even one user sees variance, trust drops.

🔧 Fix: soften guarantees into benchmarks

Example:
Instead of:

“Lead response: <60 seconds”

Use:

“Typical lead response: under 60 seconds”

or

“Designed for sub-60s response times”

You keep the wow without boxing yourself legally or technically.

🚨 3️⃣ Capacity claims may trigger disbelief

Examples:

“Unlimited tasks”

“1,000+ leads simultaneously”

“500+ pieces/month”

These are directionally fine, but skeptics may bounce.

🔧 Fix: add one limiter sentence globally

Somewhere subtle (FAQ or pricing fine print):

“Fair usage applies to prevent abuse and ensure quality for all users.”

That’s it. You don’t need to explain more.

This plan is very dense. Great for execution — but the homepage must breathe.

Rule of thumb:

Homepage: emotion + promise

Inner pages: proof + detail

Don’t dump all 7 agents’ full breakdown above the fold.
